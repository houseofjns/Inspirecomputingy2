HOUSE OF J&S COMPUTER TRAINING CENTER
YEAR 2 · BILINGUAL LEARNING PORTAL

CONTENTS
- Approved House of J&S login, dashboard and gold/navy layout
- Six Year 2 curriculum units and ten lesson notes per unit
- English and Myanmar explanations and practical activities
- 15 learning sections per unit
- Lesson visual blocks: tables, diagrams, flows, map routes and code-like commands
- Workbook answer guide from the uploaded Year 2 Workbook Answers PDF
- Practice quizzes and one 84-question / 100-mark examination per unit
- Offline-ready local assets; no source PDFs or scanned page images included

OPENING THE PORTAL
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the House-of-JS-Computing-Portal folder.
3. For basic viewing, open index.html in a modern browser.
4. For reliable offline service-worker caching, serve the folder through a small local web server (for example, Python's http.server) and visit http://localhost:8000.

SOURCE AND SCOPE NOTES
- Official Year 2 unit headings follow Pearson's Inspire Computing Course Topics document: Online services; Digital Creativity; Organising and presenting; Screen programming; Programming – Turtle World part two: the best route; Networks.
- Lesson sequences for Units 1, 2 and 6 were cross-checked against a publicly available Grade 2 scheme of work for Inspire Computing Year 2. Units 3–5 lesson labels are organised from the uploaded Year 2 Workbook Answer Sheet and course topics; where the workbook grouped lessons, the answer guide preserves grouped sections.
- The linked Scribd Student Book is identified as Inspire Computing International Year 2 Student Book (304 pages), but its full text was not available through the preview. Notes are source-aligned to the Workbook Answer Sheet and public course topics; do not treat them as a page-by-page transcription of the entire Student Book.
